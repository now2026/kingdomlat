🛡️ Golden Rules of Digital Safety

  • Encryption First: Always verify the padlock icon (HTTPS) before entering any personal data.
  • Smart Authentication: Use a dedicated password manager and enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) everywhere.
  • Privacy Browsing: Prefer privacy-respecting browsers with tracker-blocking enabled.
  • Documentation: Read privacy policies carefully and maintain control over your digital footprint.
