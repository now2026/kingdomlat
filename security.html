🔒 Privacy-First Architecture

This site is built entirely client-side. No databases, no servers processing your input, no analytics scripts. Every interaction happens locally on your device.

🚫 Zero Tracking & No Cookies

  • No session cookies, local storage abuse, or fingerprinting
  • No behavioral profiling, retargeting, or data brokers
